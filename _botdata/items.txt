6. Ammo consumption on this server: bows consume BOTH MP per normal attack (mp_consume 11-12 set on the bow item) AND arrows — org.l2jmobius Player/Creature code calls checkAndEquipArrows() and reduceArrowCount(); crossbows likewise call checkAndEquipBolts(). Inventory.findArrowForBow/findBoltForCrossBow require the ammo item's crystal grade to equal the weapon's getCrystalTypePlus(), and ItemTemplate.getCrystalTypePlus() maps both S80 and S84 weapons to grade S — so Shining Arrow (1345) works with Dynasty Bow AND Vesper Thrower, and Shining Bolt (9637) works with Dynasty Crossbow AND Vesper Shooter. Lower grades (Wooden 17/NONE, Bone 1341/D, Steel 1342/C, Silver 1343/B, Mithril 1344/A; bolts 9632-9636) would NOT match an S80/S84 weapon.
7. There are no S80- or S84-grade soulshots/spiritshots in this datapack; the single S-Grade set (1467/2514/3952) covers all S/S80/S84 weapons. Compressed packages also exist (5139 Soulshot S, 5145 Spiritshot S, 5151 Blessed Spiritshot S) if bulk loading is easier.
